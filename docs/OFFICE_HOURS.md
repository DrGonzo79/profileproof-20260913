# Office Hours: ProfileProof

Generated 2026-09-13 · Startup mode · evidence-limited draft

## Position

The premise is ethically and commercially weak as a generic “AI reputation score.” It becomes specific enough to test as a **candidate-controlled pre-application context review for nurses**. The outcome is confidence and an actionable checklist, not pretending to know what an employer will decide.

## Six forcing questions

1. **Demand reality:** The source offers a vendor-reported employer rejection statistic, not proof that candidates pay. No interviews, purchases, or observed repeated use are available. This warrants a concierge test only.
2. **Painful status quo:** A job seeker searches their name in incognito mode, scrolls years of posts across networks, asks friends what looks risky, and guesses which old content matters to a regulated role. They cannot tell completion from anxiety.
3. **Desperate specificity:** The first user is a licensed nurse changing hospitals after several years at one employer. Credentialing and background checks are consequential; an unexplained public post can create delay, embarrassment, or a rescinded offer.
4. **Narrowest paid wedge:** For $49, review user-supplied exports and return a human-verified list of no more than 10 context items, why each surfaced, and reversible next steps. No account credentials and no deletion automation.
5. **Observation plan:** Sit beside five consenting nurses while they search and triage their own public history without coaching. Record time, uncertainty, false alarms, what they refuse to change, and whether they would pay before an active application.
6. **Future-fit:** More automated employer screening increases demand for candidate visibility, but platform privacy controls and regulation could reduce the wedge. The future-fit product is an evidence-backed candidate rights and context layer, not a blacklist mirror.

## Premise challenge and alternatives

- Doing nothing produces anxiety but may not produce economic pain; paid behavior must prove urgency.
- Mirroring employer screening can amplify bias. Candidate agency, explanations, data minimization, and human review are product constraints.
- **A. Export checklist (minimal viable):** one upload, transparent phrase rules, ranked review. Effort S, risk medium; fastest demand test, weak differentiation.
- **B. Human-reviewed preflight (recommended):** rules propose items, a trained reviewer verifies context, user decides. Effort M, risk medium; credible $49 wedge and evaluation data.
- **C. Portable reputation vault (ideal architecture):** consent receipts, context statements, corrections, monitoring, and dispute workflow. Effort XL, risk high; defensible but premature.

## Assignment

Recruit five nurses actively applying this month. Observe a complete self-search, then offer the human-reviewed $49 report before building account connections. Kill the wedge if fewer than two pay or if review creates more anxiety than actionable value.
